NEAR in custody — on-chain

An independent read on the same fund: the NEAR actually sitting in Bitwise's 51 custody addresses, 48 of which delegate to bitwise_2.poolv1.near, sampled at the block nearest 16:00 ET. It confirms reported holdings and catches a stale issuer page. Note it lags share creation by roughly one session while purchases settle, and it drifts up on its own as staking rewards accrue.
